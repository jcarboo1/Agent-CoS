# Offer (MVP)
A £179, 45-minute consultation for A level and university students,
sold mainly to parents. Each client gets a one-page action plan.
A subscription may come later. (An earlier plan used £50.)
Goal: five test clients by 31 January 2027, then feedback and
testimonials, then review pricing. A three session £249 package was
considered on 22 Nov and parked (see brief.md).

# Pitch to parents
"Employers now receive around 140 applications for every graduate
job, and the best apprenticeships are just as competitive. I've sat
on the hiring side at major employers, and I know what gets
candidates through. I help your son or daughter work out what they
want, then show them how employers judge it, so their colour comes
through the paper."
(Check the latest ISE figure and its year before anything is printed.)

# Call to action
"I'm taking on just five families this winter. Book your slot
before they go."

# Framework: Be interesting, be interested
Be interesting: let your colour come through with stories only you
can tell (a job, volunteering, travel, helping a friend). Group
project examples make candidates grey because everyone has them.
Be interested: research the company and care about it.

# Three core messages
Stop mass applying. Get real experience before applications open.
Research the company properly.

# One-page action plan template
1. Where you are now: stage, goal, applications so far, strengths
   in their own words, a short chat with the parent.
2. Quick wins: two or three opportunities to go for now, plus one fix.
3. Next three steps, co-created with the student.

# Market
No local Cheltenham specialist in graduate schemes. University of
Gloucestershire Career Studio serves only its own students. Graduate
Coach charges £250 plus VAT an hour; typical UK coaching is £75 to
£250 a session. £179 sits in the upper middle of that range.
ISE: about 140 applications per graduate vacancy.

# Admin
Under £1,000 a tax year: trading allowance, no HMRC registration.
Above that: register for Self Assessment as a sole trader.
