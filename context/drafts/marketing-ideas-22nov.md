## Facebook parent group posts (parked until 3 Dec)
- Parents often ask how they can help without nagging. Ask one
  question at dinner: "What's something you sorted out yourself this
  week?" It is what assessors listen for.
- The most common thing managers say about new graduates is "They
  wait to be told." It is rarely about ability. It is a habit, and it
  can be fixed before the first interview.

## Parents' talk outline (30 minutes)
1. Hook: grades get your child shortlisted; here is what decides
   whether they are hired. Initiative, working out a task unaided,
   expecting a different job from the one they get. Anonymised
   stories only.
2. Reframe: whose job is job readiness? A gap nobody owns, and
   parents are well placed to help close it.
3. Open gap: say what assessors score, not how to prove it. Close
   with a testimonial and the invitation to the free chat.
